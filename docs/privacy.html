Privacy Policy

TaskHub Planner (“TaskHub”, “the app”, “we”)
Last updated: November 18, 2026

TaskHub Planner is a personal task planner built around the Eisenhower matrix. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what your choices are. The app is operated by Tatsiana Snapkova (“the developer”), based in Poland.

1. Data we collect

Account data. When you sign up we store your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself). If you sign in with Apple, we store your Apple user identifier and the email address Apple shares with us (which may be a private relay address).

Your content. Tasks, tags, deadlines, reminder settings, repeat rules, birthdays you enter, and your app settings (language, theme, preferences). This content is stored on our server so it is available on all your devices.

Session data. A session token is kept on your device (in the secure storage of your phone). On the server only a hash of the token is stored.

Purchase data. If you subscribe to TaskHub Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, which receives an anonymous or account-linked app user ID, your subscription status and purchase receipts so that Pro features can be unlocked. We never see or store your payment card details.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data.

3. Device features

  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Your task text is not sent to any push-notification service.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (voice input). If you use voice input, audio is processed by Apple’s speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Depending on your device and language this may happen on the device or on Apple’s servers, under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive or store your audio; only the resulting task text, if you save it.
  • Widgets. Widgets show your tasks on the home screen using data already stored on your device.

4. How we use data

  • To provide the app: sign you in, sync and store your tasks and birthdays.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock Pro features.
  • To keep the service secure (for example, rate limiting sign-in attempts) and to fix problems.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. Who processes your data

  • Hosting and database providers that run our server and store your data in the cloud.
  • Apple (sign-in with Apple, payments, speech recognition).
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription status). See revenuecat.com/privacy.

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us. Your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and content until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. The data is permanently deleted from our server. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us at the address below; we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a TaskHub Pro subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting the app from your device does not delete your server account. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required by law.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region, you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). To exercise these rights, contact us below. Our legal bases are performance of the contract (providing the app) and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

8. Children

TaskHub is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed, session tokens are stored hashed, connections use HTTPS, and every request is restricted to the signed-in user’s own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
