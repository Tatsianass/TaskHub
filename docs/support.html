Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?
Open the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. Your account, tasks and birthdays are permanently deleted from our server right away. Deleting the account does not cancel a TaskHub Pro subscription, so cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings first or afterwards. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

How do I cancel my TaskHub Pro subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose TaskHub Planner and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Sign in to the same Apple ID and use Restore Purchases in the app’s settings.
